About the Departmental Board

The Departmental Information Board was developed as part of the CIT 306 Web Development Project (Group 14). It provides a centralized digital space where students can easily access key departmental updates and academic information.

🎯 Our Purpose

To simplify departmental communication by offering a unified hub for announcements, events, timetables, and results—making it easy for students to stay informed.

💡 Key Features

  • Announcements: Stay updated with departmental news.
  • Events: View upcoming academic and social programs.
  • Timetables: Check class and exam schedules easily.
  • Results: Access uploaded results securely and promptly.

🚀 Our Vision

We aim to foster a connected academic community by leveraging technology for transparency, collaboration, and ease of access to critical departmental information.
